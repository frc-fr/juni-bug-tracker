Data origin

The data is automatically extracted from the Junos OS and Junos OS Evolved Release Notes published by Juniper Networks, a Hewlett Packard Enterprise company, on networkingdocs.hpe.com and juniper.net. Each PR links to the page of the official note mentioning it, or to the "Software Release Notification" (SRN) article of the service release, published on supportportal.juniper.net. Support dates of the branches come from Juniper's "Junos OS Dates & Milestones" page. Juniper publishes no separate table for Junos OS Evolved on that page: the same dates are applied to it, with this caveat shown. In the Hardware tab, the end of life comes from Juniper's "Hardware Dates & Milestones" tables (support.juniper.net), and the suggested release from the article KB21476 "Junos Software Versions - Suggested Releases to Consider and Evaluate" (supportportal.juniper.net).
